Okay, scene simple hai. entropy^ is a place for teens (13–19) who are curious about stuff and actually want to do something about it. A physics sim at 2am? A startup idea rotting in your notes app? A random question you can’t stop thinking about? Post it. Find people who get it, team up, and build the thing for real. No follower counts, no like-farming, no performing for an algorithm. Your profile is what you’re making and exploring, not how you look. It’s application-only so the vibe stays good, and we’re not checking your marks or your school. We just want to see that you’re curious. Half-finished idea? Perfect, that’s literally the point. Come as you are, leave with a project.
